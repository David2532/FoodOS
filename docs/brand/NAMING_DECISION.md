# FoodOS company naming decision

Status: **preliminary working decision, not legal clearance**.  
Research date: **2026-08-06**.

## Brand architecture

- Founder / owner: David Paul.
- Future legal operating company: separate corporate name.
- Product brand: FoodOS, subject to its own full trademark and market clearance.
- Future products may sit beside FoodOS under the corporate brand.

The company name must be professional, pronounceable in German and English, expandable beyond food, distinguishable and credible with customers, partners and investors.

## DP-IT assessment

**Decision: reject as preferred corporate name.**

Reasons:

- highly descriptive and weakly distinctive;
- sounds like a local IT service provider rather than a scalable product company;
- difficult to own in search, speech and trademarks;
- `dp-it.eu` is already used by an automation business;
- `DP - IT Solutions e.K.` is already publicly listed in Germany;
- several similar DP IT names exist, creating avoidable confusion.

DP-IT may remain an informal personal shorthand only. It must not be used as the public company winner without a later explicit reversal and legal review.

## Working winner

### Calyra Systems

- Suggested legal style: `Calyra Systems GmbH` after clearance and incorporation decision.
- Short name: `Calyra`.
- Pronunciation: `ka-LÜ-ra` in German or `ka-LY-ra` internationally; the final pronunciation should be standardized before launch.
- Brand idea: calm, precise systems that reduce everyday complexity.
- Relationship: `FoodOS by Calyra` or `FoodOS — a Calyra product`.
- Possible corporate tagline: **Systems for everyday clarity.**

Why it wins provisionally:

- works beyond a single food application;
- sounds like a product company rather than an IT contractor;
- supports a quiet, premium and precise visual identity;
- easy to combine with Systems, Labs or Group;
- does not describe AI, food or software so narrowly that future products become awkward.

Main risk: a broad search and official trademark/company-register similarity search has not yet been completed. The spelling may also be confused with existing names that use Caly-/Kaly- roots.

## Reserve candidates

### Velmora Labs

Strongest more expressive alternative. It supports a warm but technical portfolio identity. Risk: longer word and possible confusion with consumer/lifestyle brands.

### Kiventa Systems

Strongest enterprise-facing alternative. It sounds structured and credible. Risk: the `-venta` ending appears in multiple commercial names and may reduce distinctiveness.

## Required official next steps

Before any rename, incorporation, domain purchase or public brand spend:

1. search DPMAregister for word and figurative marks in relevant Nice classes;
2. search EUIPO/TMview and WIPO Global Brand Database for identical and similar forms;
3. search the German company register and Unternehmensregister for confusing legal names;
4. perform phonetic, spelling and stem similarity searches, not only exact matches;
5. check domains, App Store, Google Play, GitHub and major social handles;
6. have German trademark/company counsel review the final shortlist and goods/services wording;
7. record the exact search date, classes, queries and evidence;
8. obtain David's explicit approval before changing repository, company or product names.
